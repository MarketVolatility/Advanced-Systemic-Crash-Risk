Indicator explanations

D means trading days; DMA means daily moving average; bp means basis points (100 bp = 1 percentage point); pp means percentage points.

IndicatorUnitExplanation
VIXIndexExpected annualized S&P 500 volatility over the next 30 days, derived from options prices. Higher readings indicate greater expected movement, not its direction.
VIX 1D %%Percentage change in VIX from the previous trading day: (current / previous − 1) × 100. This is not the separate VIX1D index.
VIX 5D %%Percentage change in VIX over five trading days. A positive value means expected volatility has risen.
HY OASbpHigh-yield corporate bond option-adjusted spread over a Treasury benchmark, adjusted for embedded options. Wider spreads indicate greater credit stress.
HY OAS 5D bp changebpCurrent high-yield OAS minus its value five trading days ago. Positive values mean spreads widened.
HY OAS 20D bp changebpCurrent high-yield OAS minus its value twenty trading days ago. Positive values mean spreads widened.
BBB OASbpOption-adjusted spread for BBB-rated investment-grade corporate bonds. Higher readings indicate more credit risk priced into these bonds.
10Y Yield%Yield on the 10-year US Treasury. Enter 4.80 for 4.80%. This dashboard assigns more risk points to higher yields.
10Y 20D bp changebpChange in 10-year Treasury yield over twenty trading days. A move from 4.50% to 4.80% is 30 basis points.
S&P vs 50DMA%S&P 500 distance from its 50-day moving average: (index / average − 1) × 100. Negative values mean the index is below its average.
S&P vs 200DMA%S&P 500 distance from its 200-day moving average, using the same formula. Negative values indicate weakness versus the longer-term trend.
WTIUSD/barrelWest Texas Intermediate crude oil price. Use the same spot or futures series for all entries and its 20-day change. This dashboard flags high oil prices as a macro stress signal.
WTI 20D %%Percentage change in the chosen WTI price series over twenty trading days. Positive values mean oil prices rose.
MOVEIndexICE BofA bond-market implied-volatility index based on US Treasury options. Higher values indicate greater expected interest-rate volatility.
USDJPYJPY/USDJapanese yen per US dollar. A lower value means a stronger yen; this dashboard uses low levels as a carry-trade stress signal.
Breadth % >50DMA%Percentage of S&P 500 constituents above their own 50-day moving averages. Lower values indicate fewer stocks participating in the uptrend.
Breadth 20D changeppCurrent breadth percentage minus its value twenty trading days ago. A fall from 65% to 50% is −15 percentage points, not −15%.
VIX 3M-1M spreadIndex pointsVIX3M minus VIX (approximately one month). A negative result means near-term implied volatility exceeds three-month implied volatility.

Reference definitions: Cboe volatility indices · ICE MOVE · ICE bond methodology. Risk colors follow this dashboard’s existing scoring rules.

Current and past entries

Saved when you select Parse & Update or Save Manual Changes. Newest entry first. Use the red × to delete a row after confirmation. Deleting the current saved entry restores the latest remaining entry, or sample defaults if none remain. Edit a saved row’s date, time or indicator values, then select Save. Its composite score is calculated automatically from the 18 indicators. Rows are reordered by date; the newest saved row supplies the current dashboard values. Dates default to entry time and can be changed to your observation time, in , not when the market data was published. Units appear in column headings; value cells contain numbers only.

White: no risk points · Amber: intermediate risk points · Red: maximum risk points. Composite score colors: white 0–3, amber 4–11, red 12–40. Hover over a value for its score.

History syncs to your account automatically every 8 minutes while logged in (and via "Sync now"), so it follows you across devices. It's still cached locally too, so it survives a refresh even offline. Default sample values are not saved automatically.
